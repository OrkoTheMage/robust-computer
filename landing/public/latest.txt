Welcome to the first issue of Field Notes

  > I thought this was a **cool idea** — So I built it and now here we
  > are. This will be our newsletter to you, our *'News'*. Short,
  > **practical** updates from our build log. New post land when there
  > is something worth talking about: **shipped projects**, **lessons
  > learned**, **tech discovered**, or **tools we built** — like this

reading, it does not belong here. *No bull*****, no marketing copy
disguised as engineering wisdom — just the work, the mistakes, and the
small victories.
