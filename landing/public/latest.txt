  INTRODUCTION

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  WHAT TO EXPECT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

In future issues, you can look forward to tech-based topics, shipped
projects, pitfalls and lessons learned from our team. We aim to provide
valuable content for developers of all levels. No marketing copy passed
off as engineering wisdom — No bull***

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  HOW TO READ
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

You can read 'Field Notes' on the official website. Each issue will have
its own slug (e.g., issue-001) which you can access directly via its
URL. You can also read our RSS feed via XML or as plain-text.
